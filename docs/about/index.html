Based in New York

I’m Haotian Alex Xue, a graduate student in Architecture and Urban Design at Columbia University GSAPP.

My work explores architecture as a relational and adaptable framework. I’m interested in how buildings, public spaces, and ecological systems can support shared experience, preserve memory, and remain open to change.

Across design, research, exhibition, and graphic work, I investigate the boundaries between public and private, infrastructure and civic life, and the natural and constructed environment.
