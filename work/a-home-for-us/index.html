A Home for Us

A youth-centered community space that connects a new rail station with places for care and everyday public life.

Design approach

Mobility, care, and belonging.

House-like volumes form a gradient of privacy. Open programs activate the ground plane, while elevated wooden volumes accommodate counseling, studios, and health services. The proposal asks how transit investment can support existing communities.

Frosted glass, wood, and reflective metal articulate different thresholds. Rooftop gardens and sports terraces extend the idea of home into collective outdoor space, bringing mobility, care, and public life together.

Project in detail

Select an image to enlarge
01 / Design development

A community center connected to transit

The proposal brings learning, care, and recreation alongside a rail station. Classrooms, a library, music practice rooms, a dental center, and sports spaces expand the station’s role in everyday neighborhood life.

02 / Design development

A gradient of privacy

House-like volumes and connecting levels distinguish open community programs from more intimate spaces. The plans and sections trace the relationship between entrances, shared outdoor areas, and access to the train.

03 / Design development

Materials that mark transitions

Frosted glass creates a porous enclosure, timber identifies more intimate volumes, and reflective metal marks the transition toward the platform. Rooftop gardens and sports terraces extend community life outdoors.
