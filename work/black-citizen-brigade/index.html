Design approach

An identity for a local archive.

Black Citizens Brigade is presented as a cultural space and living archive. The collective proposal connects a graphic identity with systems for cataloguing, communication, and spatial adaptation.

My contribution focuses on graphic design and mock-ups, the cover and contents, and the layout and editing of the collective publication. The complete document preserves the team’s work and contribution index.

Visual identity

The graphic proposal establishes a restrained typographic system across wordmarks, labels, packaging, posters, and street-level applications. Alex Haotian Xue developed the graphic design and mock-ups, along with the publication cover, contents, layout, and editing.

A digital extension of the archive

The collective proposal treats social media as a place for cultural storytelling. Its content rhythm moves through archive objects, historical context, cultural figures, and community activation. Social media strategy by Jourdan Corker.

An archive that can be used

A shared code identifies each object by category, subcategory, condition, notes, and a unique ID. The system connects the collection to an index, labels, catalogues, and look books. Archival strategy by Ruta Hsin Yuan Hung and Roger Xingjian Tao; index by Roger Xingjian Tao; applications by Ruta Hsin Yuan Hung.

The archive in the city and the store

The urban proposal adds a canopy, projecting identity, and movable seating to strengthen the shop’s presence in the alley. Inside, a continuous modular wall combines storage and display, with elements that open and retract. Urban presence by Ryan Yihao Chen; interior and furniture design by Lillie Waddell, Rick Yida Li, Aries Jiayi Sheng, and Noor Zakaria.
