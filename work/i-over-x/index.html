10 / Graphics

I over X

A graphic identity proposal for an exhibition about self-exploration.

Year
Fall 2023
Program
Exhibition branding proposal
Design
Haotian Alex Xue
Design approach

An identity for self-exploration.

An exhibition proposal examines how social comparison shapes our sense of self. A changing typographic figure carries this inquiry across posters, registration screens, wayfinding, tickets, and merchandise.

Developed as a proposal in Fall 2023.

Self and comparison

“I” represents the self; “x” represents the many versions of the self produced through social comparison. The exhibition proposal asks visitors to reflect on the balance between self-perception and external expectations.

A changing identity

The visual identity rearranges the letterforms of “I over X” into a vertical figure. Monochrome versions establish the primary mark, while colour separates its component parts.

Promotion and registration

The identity extends into artwork-based graphics, typographic posters, and a proposed mobile registration interface.

The visitor experience

Tickets, directional graphics, and staff tags carry the same visual language into the exhibition journey.

Identity on everyday objects

Canvas bags and two T-shirt series translate the exhibition identity into objects that visitors can carry beyond the gallery.
