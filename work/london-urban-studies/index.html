08 / Research

London Urban Studies

Community research translated into modular studies, physical representations, and experiments with augmented reality.

Location
Highgate & Wood Green, London
Year
Fall 2024
Program
Urban research · Physical models · AR
Design
Haotian Alex Xue
Design approach

Neighborhoods, models, and representation.

Studies of Highgate and Wood Green examine the relationship between urban form and community life. A modular proposal for the old Highgate train station and a workplace palette emerge from neighborhood research.

Physical studies use a wheel to represent changing housing types and social composition, and strings to represent rooftop wires. AR is explored as another way to engage with design representations.
