07 / Research

London, in Print

Zine studies of the V&A Museum and Laban Dance Centre.

Location
London, United Kingdom
Year
Fall 2024
Program
Zines · Research representation
Design
Haotian Alex Xue
Design approach

Architecture observed and assembled.

Editorial layouts, photographs, and graphic studies bring architectural observations into a compact printed format. The work explores the zine as a tool for organizing and communicating research.

Produced during the Fall 2024 London study-abroad semester.

A book that becomes a space

Select an image to enlarge

Folded pages turn architectural observations into a small spatial object. Colour, transparency, and openings bring the studies of the V&A Museum and Laban Dance Centre into dialogue.

Reading architecture through light

Select an image to enlarge

The research pairs contextual analysis with personal experience, using the printed page to connect buildings, light, and perception.
