02 / Architecture

Re-Salting

A salt shed that brings municipal infrastructure, public space, and material education into a shared urban landscape.

Location
Sunset Park, Brooklyn, New York
Year
Spring 2023
Program
Infrastructure · Public space
Design
Haotian Alex Xue
Instructor
Marie Stargala
Design approach

Salt as urban landscape.

The proposal exposes the spatial and aesthetic qualities of salt: natural dunes, crystalline textures, and shifting volumes. A three-layer system preserves storage and redistribution below, introduces a public terrace in the middle, and gives the roof a silhouette informed by geological formations.

Seasonal rhythms and day–night differences organize logistical flows and public activities. The industrial facility becomes a civic interface where essential operations and everyday public experience can coexist.
