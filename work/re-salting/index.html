Design approach

Salt as urban landscape.

Re-Salting reimagines the salt shed as a hybrid urban infrastructure that merges municipal storage, public space, and material education. By exposing salt’s natural dunes, crystalline textures, and shifting volume, the project transforms an ordinarily closed industrial facility into an accessible landscape.

A three-layered vertical system preserves storage and redistribution at ground level, introduces a park-like public terrace for tactile engagement in the middle, and shapes the roof into a silhouette that echoes salt’s geological formations.

Seasonal rhythms and day–night differences separate logistical flows from public activities. The salt shed becomes a civic interface where essential operations contribute to urban culture and collective imagination.
