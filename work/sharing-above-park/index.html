Design approach

Shared space across the boundary of home.

Sharing Above Park explores how contemporary housing can renegotiate the boundary between public and private life. Lifting two residential volumes above a public platform introduces an urban ground level, a park-connected courtyard, and rooftop communal programs.

These stacked public realms create a gradient of openness between domestic intimacy, urban activity, and ecological continuity. Partially shared balconies and accessible terraces encourage encounters while preserving individual retreat.

Privacy becomes a condition shaped through proximity and shared thresholds. The project positions architecture as a mediator among people, nature, and the city, proposing a more interconnected model of urban living.

Layers of public and private life

Two residential bars sit above a public platform. The exploded axonometric and program diagram trace the gradient from public to semi-public, semi-private, and private space.

Balconies as shared thresholds

Large units share balconies across floors, with opportunities to connect neighbors. Smaller units pair individual north-facing balconies with a shared southern balcony.
