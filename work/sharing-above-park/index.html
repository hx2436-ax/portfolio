Sharing Above Park

A residential complex exploring the changing boundary between public and private life.

Location
Amsterdam, Netherlands
Year
Fall 2023
Program
Housing · Public space
Design
Haotian Alex Xue
Instructor
Richard Rosa
Design approach

A shared ground for urban living.

Two residential volumes are lifted above a public platform. An urban ground level, a courtyard connected to the park, and communal rooftop programs create a sequence of shared spaces between domestic life and the city.

Partially shared balconies and accessible terraces encourage encounters while retaining places for individual retreat. Privacy is approached as a condition shaped through proximity and thresholds, with architecture mediating among people, nature, and urban activity.
