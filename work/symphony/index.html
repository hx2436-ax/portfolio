01 / Architecture

Symphony of Wind, Light and Life

A post-war urban seed library and public landscape that reconnects people with nature, memory, and one another.

Location
Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina
Year
Spring 2024
Program
Public landscape · Seed library
Design
Haotian Alex Xue & Yihao Ryan Chen
Instructor
Christina Chi Zhang
Design approach

Wind, light, and collective life.

An underused urban space becomes a sensory journey through three experiences: the Wind Walk, the Light Pavilion, and the Garden Walk. Valley winds, shifting light, and Sarajevo’s tradition of home gardening inform spaces for play, rest, cultivation, and collective gathering.

A living seed library invites residents to exchange, grow, and learn from local plants. Ecological renewal becomes a shared civic practice, with public space supporting everyday encounters and collective healing.
