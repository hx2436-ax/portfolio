A poem for the National and University Library of Bosnia and Herzegovina

A pine seed—this is where life begins.
With time, you grow in quiet and peace,
rooting the mountains in life.

A book—this is where knowledge begins.
With time, wisdom gathers, abundant and alive,
nourishing the strength of the human spirit.

Then, in 1992, war arrives.
Relentless shelling and merciless axes
tear through lives grown over generations.
People flee through a rain of bullets;
with your fragile trunks, you shelter them.
The trees fall. Despair fills the sky.
For four long years, lives beyond counting are lost.

Then, in 1992, war arrives.
Deliberate fire and devastating bombardment
consume the memory and wisdom of generations.
Pages burn. Amid the flames,
people rush to save the books that remain.
The library stands hollow, a sanctuary reduced to ruins.
Even its walls can no longer offer shelter.

When war ends, your bark becomes an image of healing,
tending the wounds the body carries.
Among fallen trees, seeds awaken.
From nature, we receive love.

When war ends, the words that survive
begin to tend the wounds the heart carries.
Among the ruins, people sing of freedom.
From the world, we receive love.

The river beats like a heart,
carrying water and nourishment through the forest.
Life returns to the trees.

The river beats like a heart,
circulating life through the city.
Books return to the library.

下排 / 图书馆

写给波斯尼亚和黑塞哥维那国家与大学图书馆的诗

一颗松子，是生命开始的地方。
岁月流转，你在安详与和平中生长，
让群山成为生命栖息的家园。

一本书，是知识开始的地方。
岁月流转，智慧不断积累，丰盛而鲜活，
成为人们精神力量的源泉。

然而，1992年，战争侵袭了这里。
无尽的炮火与无情的砍伐，
夺走了你们历经岁月的生命。
人们在如雨的子弹中奔逃，
你以脆弱的树干，为无助的人们遮挡危险。
树木倒下，绝望悬在空中；
漫长的四年里，无数生命消逝。

然而，1992年，战争侵袭了这里。
蓄意的纵火与猛烈的轰炸，
吞噬了世代积累的记忆与智慧。
书页在烈火中燃烧，
人们奔向火场，抢救尚存的书籍。
图书馆只剩空壳，知识的圣地化为废墟；
就连建筑，也无法再为人们提供庇护。

战争结束，树皮成为疗愈的意象，
抚慰人们身体上的创伤。
种子在枯木之间，重新萌发。
我们从自然中获得爱。

战争结束，幸存书页中的力量，
开始抚慰人们心灵上的创伤。
人们在废墟之间，歌唱自由。
我们从世界中获得爱。

河流如同心脏，
将水分与养分送往森林。
生命，重回林间。

河流如同心脏，
让生机重新流转于城市。
书，回到了图书馆。

Design approach

Wind, light, and collective life.

An underused urban space becomes a sensory journey through three experiences: the Wind Walk, the Light Pavilion, and the Garden Walk. Valley winds, shifting light, and Sarajevo’s tradition of home gardening inform spaces for play, rest, cultivation, and collective gathering.

A living seed library invites residents to exchange, grow, and learn from local plants. Ecological renewal becomes a shared civic practice, with public space supporting everyday encounters and collective healing.
