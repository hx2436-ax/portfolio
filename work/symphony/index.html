A poem for the National and University Library of Bosnia and Herzegovina

A pine seed—this is where life begins.
With time, you grow in quiet and peace,
rooting the mountains in life.

A book—this is where knowledge begins.
With time, wisdom gathers, abundant and alive,
nourishing the strength of the human spirit.

Then, in 1992, war arrives.
Relentless shelling and merciless axes
tear through lives grown over generations.
People flee through a rain of bullets;
with your fragile trunks, you shelter them.
The trees fall. Despair fills the sky.
For four long years, lives beyond counting are lost.

Then, in 1992, war arrives.
Deliberate fire and devastating bombardment
consume the memory and wisdom of generations.
Pages burn. Amid the flames,
people rush to save the books that remain.
The library stands hollow, a sanctuary reduced to ruins.
Even its walls can no longer offer shelter.

When war ends, your bark becomes an image of healing,
tending the wounds the body carries.
Among fallen trees, seeds awaken.
From nature, we receive love.

When war ends, the words that survive
begin to tend the wounds the heart carries.
Among the ruins, people sing of freedom.
From the world, we receive love.

The river beats like a heart,
carrying water and nourishment through the forest.
Life returns to the trees.

The river beats like a heart,
circulating life through the city.
Books return to the library.

Design approach
Design approach

A story of people, nature, and collective memory.

Rooted in Sarajevo’s complex relationship between people, nature, and collective memory, Symphony of Wind, Light and Life begins with a storybook exploring how pine trees and residents protected one another during the war, while the river sustained the city as its heartbeat.

Inspired by these narratives, the project seeks to restore the fading connections between urban life and nature. Transforming an underused parking lot between commercial and residential neighborhoods, the design introduces interconnected public spaces: wind-animated streets, a tranquil water pavilion, and a community garden with a living seed library.

Through encounters with wind, water, plants, and one another, the project creates an evolving urban living room that celebrates Sarajevo’s cultural identity, encourages spontaneous public activities, and reconnects everyday life with the natural world.

Research, design, and three sensory journeys

Select an image to enlarge

From urban history to gradual regeneration

Site history, community needs, and the relationship between the city and its landscape establish a framework for renewing an underused parking lot.

Wind Walk

Suspended fabrics respond to the wind, creating shifting shadows and movement. Wind chimes surrounding a central tree translate invisible breezes into sound. Play, chess, exhibitions, graffiti, and public gatherings allow residents to continually reshape the streets.

Light Pavilion

At the convergence of two pedestrian journeys, a lightweight pavilion offers a sheltered urban living room. Water flowing across the roof creates reflections and shifting light, inviting visitors to pause, read, share tea, and exchange stories.

Garden Walk

The hillside garden becomes a living collection shaped by seasonal change and local participation. Double-sided modular walls hold seed archives, educational displays, compost, and tools. Taking seeds home extends ecological connections into the surrounding city.

One continuous public landscape

A masterplan, urban installations, a sequence of moments, and a long section connect the three experiences into a continuous pedestrian journey.
