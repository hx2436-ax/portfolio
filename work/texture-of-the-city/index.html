Texture of the City

An exhibition bringing together artwork, architectural research, and observations of Shanghai’s urban texture.

Location
Shanghai, China
Year
2020
Program
Exhibition · Art · Urban heritage
Design
Haotian Alex Xue / Issue Studio
Design approach

Reading the city through its traces.

The exhibition connects personal readings of the city with collective research on Shikumen heritage. Mixed-media work, postcards, spatial studies, and exhibition design offer different ways of looking at the city and its everyday traces.

The portfolio documents individual artwork alongside group research and exhibition work developed through Issue Studio.
