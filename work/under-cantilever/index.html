05 / Architecture

Under Cantilever

A building material reclamation center designed as a durable framework for changing uses and environmental conditions.

Location
South Boston, Massachusetts
Year
Spring 2025
Program
Material reuse · Adaptive infrastructure
Design
Haotian Alex Xue & Yihao Ryan Chen
Instructor
Il Wan Kim
Design approach

A long life for materials and space.

Prestressed and post-tensioned concrete enable a large cantilever and flexible, column-free space for receiving, sorting, and redistributing reclaimed materials. Separate circulation accommodates trucks and processing at ground level, with offices above.

The design studies how a durable structure could adapt as sea levels and urban needs change. A sequence of possible futures—from reclamation center to marketplace to pier—extends reuse from individual materials to the life of an entire building.
