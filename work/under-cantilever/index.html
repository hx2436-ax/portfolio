Design approach

A long life for materials and space.

“Under Cantilever” redefines reuse as both a material practice and a long-term architectural strategy. Using prestressed and post-tensioned concrete, the project creates a vast cantilevered volume that enables column-free flexibility for receiving, sorting, and redistributing reclaimed materials.

Clear circulation separates industrial operations from administrative functions: trucks access the southern ground level for loading and processing, while staff enter from the north and ascend directly to the upper office zone.

Beyond its current purpose, the structure anticipates future ecological shifts. As sea levels rise or urban programs evolve, the durable concrete frame can be adapted rather than demolished, serving as an armature for new environmental or infrastructural uses. The reclamation center is a long-lived spatial resource linking material reuse, ecological resilience, and architectural longevity.

01 / Design development

Reuse across different timescales

The project considers a three-foot sea-level-rise scenario for South Boston in 2070. Alongside this site study, material lifespan comparisons and a concrete reclamation cycle frame reuse as a response to both environmental change and the different rates at which materials age.

02 / Design development

A durable frame with room to change

Structural studies connect the internal system and beam unit with model testing, precast production, and on-site assembly. The exploded axonometric shows how these components form a large cantilever and a flexible ground-level framework.

03 / Design development

Separating industrial and staff movement

Trucks enter from the south for loading and processing. Staff enter from the north and ascend to the second-floor offices, keeping administrative circulation distinct from the movement of reclaimed materials.

04 / Design development

From structural system to inhabited space

The chunk study connects structural components and enclosure. Model views then move through the woodshop, storage area, material library, and second-floor office, showing how the same framework supports different spatial conditions.

05 / Design development

Three proposed futures

The project imagines a material reclamation center today, a marketplace after 30 years, and a pier after 70 years. These design scenarios pair increasing water levels with new uses for the retained structural frame.
